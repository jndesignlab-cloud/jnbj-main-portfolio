# DesignLab Portfolio v3.12.0 — One-Stop Services & Localized Pricing

- Replaced the old guided/tabbed services layout with a single classified service grid.
- Categories: Social & Campaigns; Brand, Print & Packaging; Websites & Digital; Add-ons & Custom.
- All standard rates increased by at least 10%.
- PHP remains the studio base rate.
- Approximate country detection uses ipapi.co.
- Currency conversion uses open.er-api.com.
- If either lookup fails, prices fall back to PHP + approximate USD.
- A manual PHP/USD toggle remains available after local pricing is detected.
- Final rates are still confirmed after the brief is reviewed.
